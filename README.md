# RFM model- customer segmentation

We calculate the RFM score by attributing a numerical value for each of the criteria. 
The customer gets more points -
  1. if they bought in the recent past, 
  2. bought many times or 
  3. if the purchase value is larger. 
Three values are combined to create the RFM score.
This RFM score can then be used to segment your customer data platform (CDP).
Customer RFM scores will be used to create 11 segments and these segments will be used to create specified marketing strategies.  which are-

1. CHAMPIONS: Bought recently, buy often and spend the most. These are early adopters for new products and will promote the brand.
2. LOYAL CUSTOMERS: Spend good money with us often. responsive to promotions. Upsell higher value products, hence, ask for reviews and engage them.
3. POTENTIAL LOYALIST: recent customers, but spend a good amount and bought more than once. Hence, offer membership/ loyalty program, recommend other products.
4. RECENT CUSTOMERS: Bought most recently but not often. Hence, provide on-boarding support, give them early success, start building relationship.
5. PROMISING: recent shoppers, but haven't spent much. Hence, create brand awareness and offer free trials.
6. CUSTOMER NEEDING ATTENTION: above average RFM values, may not have bought very recently. Hence, make limited time offers. recommend based on past purchases and reactivate them.
7. ABOUT TO SLEEP: below average RFM values. will lose them if not reactivated. Hence, share valuable resources, recommend popular products/ renewals at discount, reconnect with them.
8. AT RISK: Spent big money and purchased often but long time ago and need to bring them back. Hence, send personalized emails to reconnect, offer renewals, provide helpful resources.
9. CAN'T LOSE THEM: Made biggest purchases and often but haven't returned for a long time. Hence, win them back via renewals or newer products and don't lose them to competition, talk to them.
10. HIBERNATING: last purchase was long back, low spenders and low number of orders. Hence, offer other relevant products and special discounts.
11. LOST: lowest RFM values. revive interest with reach out campaign or ignore otherwise.
